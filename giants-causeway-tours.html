    Giant’s Causeway & Antrim Coast Tour

    An unforgettable full-day private journey along one of the world's greatest coastal drives. 40,000 ancient basalt columns, dramatic castle ruins, and secluded Atlantic harbours.

    Book Causeway Tour Direct Tour Line: 07894270520
    UNESCO World Heritage

    The Legend of Finn McCool

    Rising from the cold depths of the North Atlantic Ocean, the Giant's Causeway is an awe-inspiring geological phenomenon consisting of 40,000 interlocking polygonal basalt columns, formed 60 million years ago by volcanic activity—or, as Irish legend tells it, carved by the mighty giant Finn McCool to battle his Scottish rival Benandonner.

    On your private tour, there are no rushed queues or crowded buses. Your knowledgeable local chauffeur guides you to premier viewpoints, shares folklore and geological insight, and arranges reservations at authentic coastal inns like The Bushmills Inn.

    Flexible 8 to 9 Hour Journey: Depart directly from your hotel, cruise ship, or airport.
    All Major Highlights Included: Dunluce Castle, Carrick-a-Rede, Ballintoy, Bushmills, and Dark Hedges.
    Luxury Cabin Comfort: Mercedes-Benz S-Class or V-Class with chilled mineral water and onboard Wi-Fi.
    Curated Itinerary

    Your Private Day Itinerary

    01

    Carrickfergus Castle & Coast

    Begin by sweeping past the shores of Belfast Lough to visit 12th-century Carrickfergus Castle before joining the dramatic Antrim Coast Road.

    02

    Carnlough & Glens of Antrim

    Traverse the picturesque Glens, stopping at Carnlough's limestone harbour and Cushendun's rugged ocean caves.

    03

    Carrick-a-Rede & Ballintoy

    Spectacular vistas over the 66-foot chasm to Rathlin Island, followed by the enchanting harbour of Ballintoy (Iron Islands in Game of Thrones).

    04

    Giant's Causeway Exploration

    2 full unhurried hours exploring the basalt hexagonal amphitheatre, wishing chair, and dramatic coastal cliff trails.

    05

    Dunluce Castle Ruins

    Perched dramatically on the basalt cliff edge. Photograph the medieval seat of the Clan MacDonnell and listen to tales of the kitchen that fell into the sea.

    06

    The Dark Hedges

    Conclude your day along the mesmerizing 18th-century avenue of intertwined beech trees (the iconic Kingsroad), before a serene journey back to Belfast.
