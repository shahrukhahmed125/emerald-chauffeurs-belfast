    Belfast City & Titanic Quarter Tour

    Immerse yourself in Belfast's rich industrial story, Victorian architectural splendor, world-class Titanic heritage, and vibrant cultural quarters.

    Book City Tour Concierge: 07894270520
    Capital City Heritage

    The Maritime Capital Revealed

    Once known as the shipbuilding and linen capital of the British Empire, Belfast is a city of remarkable resilience, architectural grandeur, and warmth. On our private tour, you travel in the quiet luxury of an executive Mercedes-Benz with a knowledgeable local chauffeur who brings the city's story vividly to life.

    Visit Titanic Belfast, standing on the very slipways where RMS Titanic was conceived, built, and launched. Explore the historic Drawing Offices where naval architects drafted her blueprints, see the SS Nomadic tender ship, and gaze up at Harland & Wolff's yellow gantry cranes, Samson and Goliath.

    Flexible Duration: Available as a concise 4-hour highlights tour or a comprehensive 7-hour in-depth exploration.
    Door-to-Door Pickups: Direct collection from any hotel, cruise port terminal, or airport in Belfast.
    Belfast Castle Viewpoint: Panoramic sweep across the entire city basin and Belfast Lough.
    Key Landmarks

    Signature Sights on Your Private Tour

    Titanic Quarter & Slipways

    Stand where Titanic touched the water in 1911. Visit the shimmering aluminum-clad Titanic Belfast, SS Nomadic, and the Great Light.

    Belfast City Hall & Donegall Square

    Stunning classical Baroque Portland stone civic building opened in 1906, featuring the Titanic Memorial Garden and ornate stained glass rotunda.

    Queen’s University & Botanic Gardens

    The striking Tudor Gothic Lanyon Building in South Belfast's leafy University Quarter, accompanied by the Victorian curvilinear iron Palm House.
